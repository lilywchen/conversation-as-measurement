# Security and privacy

This project is designed for public, aggregate, or synthetic inputs. Do not submit or
commit protected health information (PHI), personally identifying clinical content,
credentials, private service URLs, or model outputs that quote real encounters.

If you discover a credential or potentially identifying clinical content in the
repository, do not open a public issue. Contact the maintainers privately at
`l1ly@stanford.edu` with the affected path and minimal non-sensitive context. Do not copy
the sensitive content into the report.
